importance_pageRank.py 

This script scores the paper at paragraph level using two channels:
- T(p): technical value of the paragraph itself (LLM, citation-agnostic)
- C(p): extra value added by citations in that paragraph

It then runs PageRank-style propagation on a paper citation graph:
- technical channel -> TR
- citation channel -> CR

After that, paper-level TR/CR are pushed back down to paragraphs in proportion to local T(p) and C(p).  
Final paragraph score is a blend:
combined = lambda * technical_rank + (1 - lambda) * citation_rank

Section output is a hierarchy where top-level section scores are normalized to sum to 1.


How this differs from importance_score.py

importance_score.py:
- Uses direct/pairwise LLM comparisons to allocate scores across sections/subsections/paragraphs.
- Assigns citation scores by splitting paragraph score across citation mentions.
- Does not run graph propagation (no PageRank step).

importance_pageRank.py:
- Separates technical and citation channels explicitly (T and C).
- Adds graph propagation (TR and CR) and then redistribution to paragraphs.
- Produces extra outputs for paper-level decomposition and normalized hierarchical section scores.
