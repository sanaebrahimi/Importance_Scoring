PageRank scoring guide (short version)

Local paragraph scoring

For each paragraph p, the model provides:
- S_local(p): overall local contribution in [0,1]
- t_raw(p): technical raw strength (>=0)
- c_raw(p): citation-added raw strength (>=0)

We normalize raw strengths:
- w_t = t_raw / (t_raw + c_raw)
- w_c = c_raw / (t_raw + c_raw)

Then split local total:
- T(p) = S_local(p) * w_t
- C(p) = S_local(p) * w_c

So T(p) + C(p) = S_local(p).
If p has no citation mentions, we force C(p)=0 and T(p)=S_local(p).

Internal graph for propagation
- paper -> section/subsection -> paragraph -> citation leaves
- Paragraphs without citations are leaves.

Propagation equations (for every node u)
- TR_{t+1}(u) = (1-alpha)*T(u) + alpha*sum_{v->u} TR_t(v)/out_deg(v)
- CR_{t+1}(u) = (1-beta)*C(u) + beta*sum_{v->u} CR_t(v)/out_deg(v)

Flow meaning
- Edge v -> u means v sends part of its propagated score to u.
- out_deg(v) is number of outgoing edges of v.
- v splits flow equally across outgoing edges.

Final paragraph score
- combined_score(p) = lambda*technical_rank(p) + (1-lambda)*citation_rank(p)

Output files

run_pagerank_paragraph_scores.json
- local_total, local_technical, local_citation
- technical_rank, citation_rank
- combined_score
- normalized_combined_score

run_pagerank_section_scores.json
- hierarchical section tree
- score normalized so top-level sections sum to 1
- parent score equals sum of children scores

run_pagerank_citation_scores.json
- citation-level aggregation from citation leaf nodes
- technical_rank/citation_rank/combined_score per citation

run_pagerank_paper_scores.json
- local paper totals before propagation
- propagated paper technical_rank and citation_rank
- decomposition into kept_local and inherited_flow
- graph details (nodes, edges, metadata)
