
This run uses two local paragraph channels plus propagation:

1) Local paragraph scores
- T(p): technical contribution from LLM (citation-agnostic).
- S_local(p): overall paragraph contribution from LLM.
- C(p): citation-added value.

Equations:
- C(p) = max(0, S_local(p) - T(p))
- If paragraph p has no citation mentions: C(p) = 0 and S_local(p) is set to T(p)
- So for every paragraph in output: local_total = local_technical + local_citation

2) Paper-level propagation (citation graph)
For paper node u:
- TR_{t+1}(u) = (1-alpha)*T(u) + alpha*sum_{v->u} TR_t(v)/out_deg(v)
- CR_{t+1}(u) = (1-beta)*C(u) + beta*sum_{v->u} CR_t(v)/out_deg(v)

3) Paragraph redistribution from paper-level ranks
For paragraph p in paper P:
- technical_rank(p) = TR(P) * T(p) / sum_{q in P} T(q)
- citation_rank(p)  = CR(P) * C(p) / sum_{q in P} C(q)

4) Final combined score
- combined_score(p) = lambda * technical_rank(p) + (1-lambda) * citation_rank(p)

Why local_citation is often 0
- C(p) is only the part above technical score: max(0, S_local - T).
- If the overall scorer does not give more than T(p), C(p) becomes 0.
- Paragraphs without citation mentions are forced to C(p)=0.

Output files

run_pagerank_paragraph_scores.json
- local_total: S_local(p)
- local_technical: T(p)
- local_citation: C(p)
- technical_rank: redistributed TR(p)
- citation_rank: redistributed CR(p)
- combined_score: weighted blend of technical_rank and citation_rank
- normalized_combined_score: paragraph contribution normalized so all paragraphs in paper sum to 1

run_pagerank_section_scores.json
- Hierarchical tree (sections/subsections)
- score: normalized contribution (top-level sections sum to 1)
- raw_combined_score: unnormalized sum of paragraph combined_score under that node
- technical_rank / citation_rank: sums of paragraph technical_rank / citation_rank under that node

run_pagerank_paper_scores.json
- local.technical / local.citation / local.overall_total: paper local values before propagation
- technical_rank / citation_rank: propagated paper-level values
- decomposition.technical and decomposition.citation:
  kept_local = (1-damping)*local, inherited_flow = damping*incoming_flow

run_pagerank_citation_scores.json
- Citation-level aggregation from paragraph mentions
- technical_rank and citation_rank come from splitting paragraph ranks over that paragraph's mentions
- combined_score is the same lambda blend at citation level
